# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to
help.

## What is expected

- Be respectful, patient and generous.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and that a maintainer may decide differently
  from you.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Other conduct that would be unacceptable in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report anything that breaks these rules to the maintainer. Reports are handled
privately and reviewed. Maintainers who do not follow this policy in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as appropriate.
